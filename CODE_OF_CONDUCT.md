# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory or sexualized language are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through GitHub:
[open a private security advisory](https://github.com/shayan-m81/layerwright/security/advisories/new) and mark it as
a conduct report, or message [@shayan-m81](https://github.com/shayan-m81). All reports are reviewed and kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that violate this code, and may
ban contributors temporarily or permanently.
